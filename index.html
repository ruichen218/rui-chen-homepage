About my research

About

Welcome to my personal webpage. My research lies in analysis and geometry, with a focus on nonlocal operators such as the fractional Laplacian, the logarithmic Laplacian, and the fractional-logarithmic Laplacian. I am particularly interested in the partial differential equations, functional inequalities, and spectral theory associated with these operators, especially in the settings of manifolds and discrete graphs.

I’m always happy to chat about mathematics, exchange ideas, and explore possible collaborations.
