About

Welcome to my personal webpage. My research lies in analysis and geometry, with a focus on nonlocal operators such as the fractional Laplacian, the logarithmic Laplacian, and the fractional-logarithmic Laplacian. I am particularly interested in the partial differential equations, functional inequalities, and spectral theory associated with these operators, especially in the settings of manifolds and discrete graphs.

I’m always happy to chat about mathematics, exchange ideas, and explore possible collaborations.

Research Interests

Main topics currently shaping my work.

Nonlocal Operators

Fractional Laplacians, logarithmic Laplacians, and related nonlocal operators on Euclidean spaces, Riemannian manifolds, and discrete graphs.

Spectral Theory and Geometric Analysis

Eigenvalue problems, sharp inequalities, spectral asymptotics, conformal geometry and other geometry phenomena in nonlocal settings.

Partial Differential Equations

Existence, regularity, asymptotic analysis, variational methods, and Brezis-Nirenberg problems for nonlocal equations.
